Indicator 1 (Weight: 35%)

Local Topographic Depressions / Sinks

Computed via morphological reconstruction sink filling on the 1m NOAA Bare Earth DEM. Identifies natural hollows and enclosed terrain bowls where overland water collects before reaching a spillover threshold.

Limitation & DEM Artifacts: Highway overpasses (e.g., I-45, Gulf Freeway) or bridges without burned-in culverts appear as solid terrain dams in raw LiDAR, potentially creating false depression sinks. Does not account for unmapped culverts.
Indicator 2 (Weight: 25%)

Topographic Position Index (TPI / Relative Low)

Compares pixel elevation against the mean elevation within a 30-meter moving neighborhood. Negative TPI values isolate localized swales, roadside ditches, and drainage pathways.

Indicator 3 (Weight: 20%)

Surface Slope & Flatness

Evaluates terrain gradient. Slopes below 1.5° (covering 46% of the pilot area) drastically retard gravity-driven runoff evacuation, prolonging street-level ponding retention.

Indicator 4 (Weight: 20%)

Drainage Proximity (Distance to Nearest Storm Inlet)

Calculated as true 2D Euclidean distance in meters (using projected metric coordinates EPSG:6344) to the 265 verified municipal storm inlets. Mean distance is 54.7m.

Limitation: Proximity is a screening proxy only. It does not verify underground pipe capacity, conduit slopes (86.2% unrecorded), flow direction, inlet grate clogging, or bayou backwater submergence.

Scientific Distinctions & Physical Regimes

Regime Mismatch

Pluvial vs. Fluvial Flooding

M1 evaluates pluvial overland ponding governed by micro-depressions and distance to municipal storm inlets during heavy cloudbursts. Available high-water marks (USGS) document fluvial riverine crests in the Buffalo Bayou channel. Conflating the two invalidates scientific validation.

Integrity Rule

Modeled Zones vs. Observed Reality

FEMA 100-year and 500-year flood hazard layers are derived from steady-state numerical models. Validating an exploratory screening index against a regulatory simulation is model-to-model cross-comparison, not empirical ground-truth validation.

Location Artifact

311 Geocoding Clustering at City Hall

In City of Houston 311 logs, calls lacking specific street addresses default to Houston City Hall (901 Bagby St) inside the pilot area. Evaluating against 311 records would measure municipal phone routing rather than physical hydrology.

Required Missing Evidence for Future Empirical Validation

  • Calibrated In-Situ Water Depth Sensors: High-frequency IoT ultrasonic or pressure transducer sensors in street gutters or curb inlets logging water depths (cm/in).
  • High-Resolution Spatial Inundation Polygons: Aerial orthophotography or satellite optical/SAR imagery captured during peak storm cloudbursts over the 0.97 km² grid.
  • Event-Synchronized Time-Series: Timestamped waterlogging onset and recession logs aligned with 15-minute gauge records (HCFWS Station 1600).
  • Operational State of Drainage Inlets: Empirical field records of inlet grate clogging and bayou backwater submergence.
