The Difference Between Static Maps and Dynamic Decision Support

Traditional static flood hazard zones fail during dynamic cloudbursts. When streets turn into storm canals, emergency responders need real-time clarity, not outdated 100-year flood zone PDFs.

The Conventional Flaw

Traditional Flood Maps Blindfold Dispatchers

Conventional municipal flood maps are designed for long-term insurance zoning, not operational dispatch during flash storm surges.

  • Static 100-Year Zones: Zero timestep visibility during rapid 50mm–100mm/hr intense cloudbursts.
  • Ignored Drainage Conduits: Disregards whether underground storm mains are flowing freely or back-surcharging.
  • Shortest-Distance Traps: Commercial GPS routes ambulances into submerged underpasses and drowned access ramps.
The AquaPredict Solution

Deterministic Physics & Flood-Aware Routing

AquaPredict synthesizes high-fidelity topography, drainage capacity, and lexicographic Dijkstra routing into one unified command center.

  • Dynamic M5 Inundation: 36 discrete 5-minute timestep depth rasters modeled under calibrated rainfall intensity curves.
  • Drainage-Informed Network (M6): Explicit modeling of inlets, gravity mains, and outfalls to detect hydraulic surcharge bottlenecks.
  • Safest Route Planner (M7): Lexicographic Dijkstra route optimization that strictly excludes impassable roads (≥0.30m depth).

Methodology & Architecture

How AquaPredict Operates

A transparent 4-stage pipeline that converts raw spatial geometry into vetted operational rescue routes.

01

LiDAR Topography & Inlets

We ingest sub-meter DEM elevation data alongside City of Houston municipal stormwater shapefiles, mapping gravity mains and outfall discharge points.

02

Physical Rainfall Screening

Scenario presets (10mm to 100mm/hr) run through our physical screening surrogate to compute transient surface water accumulation across 36 timesteps.

03

Access Corridor Auditing

Rescue Priority Predictor verifies all access roads connecting hospitals and communities, automatically flagging road failure and isolation risks.

04

Dijkstra Decision Routing

The Safest Route Planner evaluates candidate routes, penalizing unmeasured sections and strictly excluding impassable segments for vehicles and pedestrians.

Scientific Scope & Disclaimer

AquaPredict's M5 flood depths are computed via a physical screening surrogate model. Route recommendations from the Safest Route Planner are scenario-based decision support estimates and must NOT be interpreted as certified safe evacuation routing or official civil defense clearance.
